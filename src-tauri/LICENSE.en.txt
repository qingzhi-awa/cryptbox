CryPtBox — Terms of Use & Privacy Statement

1. Software Nature
CryPtBox is open-source software. All source code of the client and server is publicly
available for review and audit. Anyone with technical skills can inspect the code to
confirm that the software does not upload, steal, or leak your data.

2. Fully Local Operation
The CryPtBox server runs on your own fnOS device. All data (password vault, account
information, etc.) is stored in a local database on your device, and is never uploaded
to the developer or any third-party server.

3. Password Security
- Password entries are encrypted with AES-256-GCM before being written to the database.
- The encryption key is stored only on your own device.
- The developer cannot access your device, and therefore cannot access your passwords or any data.

4. Your Data Belongs to You
Your data belongs only to you. When uninstalling the software, you may choose to keep
or permanently delete all data.

5. Disclaimer
CryPtBox is provided "as is". The developer is not liable for any data loss or damage
caused by the use of this software.

6. It is recommended that you back up important data regularly to prevent data loss.
CryPtBox does not provide compensation for data loss. The final right of interpretation
belongs to the CryPtBox development team.
